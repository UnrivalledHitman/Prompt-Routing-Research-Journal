\section*{Vitae}

\noindent\textbf{Indrajeet Mondal} carried out this work during his Master of
Computer Applications (2024--2026) in the Department of Computer Application
at Narula Institute of Technology, Kolkata, India. He received his B.Sc.\
(Hons) in Computer Science from St.\ Xavier's College, Kolkata, in 2024. From
February to August 2025 he worked at AEITY Systems as a web developer and
later team lead, building and deploying the company's production website on
AWS. His earlier research projects covered crop-yield prediction and stress
detection from wearable-sensor data. His research interests include machine
learning, deep learning and efficient inference for large language models.

\bigskip

\noindent\textbf{Suseta Datta} has been an Assistant Professor in the
Department of Computer Application at Narula Institute of Technology,
Kolkata, India, since July 2024. Before that she worked at the University of
Engineering and Management, Kolkata, for two years, and gained industry
experience as a full-stack developer. She received her M.Tech in Computer
Science and Engineering from the University of Kalyani in 2021. She has
published papers at IEEE and Springer conferences and has published patents.
Her research interests include artificial intelligence, machine learning and
blockchain.
